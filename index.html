About Us

Kashmiri Shawl House is a family business located in Kotwali Bazaar near Clock Tower, Hoshiarpur. Established in 1995, we specialize in authentic Kashmiri shawls, embroidered collections and premium winter wear sourced directly from Kashmir. We proudly ship across India and internationally.

The Artistry Gallery

Each piece reflects the heritage and craftsmanship of Kashmir.

A Legacy Since 1995

For over three decades, Kashmiri Shawl House has been preserving the timeless beauty of authentic Kashmiri craftsmanship. What began as a family business in Hoshiarpur has grown through generations of trust, quality, and a passion for bringing the elegance of Kashmir to customers across India and around the world.
