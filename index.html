OUR STORY · SINCE 1995

From Kashmir,
with warmth.

Kashmiri Shawl House was built around a love for Kashmiri textiles and a desire to share their beauty with more people. Since 1995, our journey has been about connecting customers with shawls and winter wear that celebrate tradition.

From everyday favourites to special pieces, we believe every customer deserves personal attention and an honest shopping experience.
